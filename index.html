Why we're called Wall of Okay

That wall in front of you has a name.

ADHD coach Brendan Mahan calls it the Wall of Awful. Every late reply, every "why can't you just…", every time it went wrong stacks up into a wall between you and the task. The bigger it gets, the harder it is to start. That's not laziness. That's a wall.

We can't knock it down for you. But we can make the first step small enough to climb, then the next one, until you're on the other side. Not perfect. Just okay.
